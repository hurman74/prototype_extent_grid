## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `SliverPrototypeExtentGrid` with `.builder` and `.list` constructors: a sliver grid whose
  cells take the size of an offstage prototype item.
- `PrototypeExtentGridView` with default, `.builder` and `.custom` constructors.
- `PrototypeGridColumns.count`, `.maxExtent` and `.fit` column strategies.
- Exact scroll extent from the first frame for grids with a known item count.
